The premise

Searching without a map

Metaheuristics find good-enough solutions to hard problems where brute force is impossible and gradients don't exist. They share one core tension:

  • Exploration — venture into unknown regions of the search space
  • Exploitation — refine the promising areas already found
  • No gradients required — they only need to evaluate fitness
  • Stochastic by design — randomness escapes local traps

01 · Genetic Algorithm

Survival of the fittest

How it works

  • Selection — fitter parents are chosen to breed
  • Crossover — parents swap genetic material
  • Mutation — random tweaks keep variety alive
  • Elitism — the best survive untouched

Character

Aggressive exploiter. Converges fast and finds excellent solutions — but elitist pressure can collapse diversity and trap it on a single peak.

02 · Bee Colony Optimization

The wisdom of the hive

How it works

  • Scouts — explore the space at random
  • Waggle dance — good finds recruit others
  • Recruitment — bees swarm to rich sources
  • Abandonment — exhausted sources are dropped

Character

Patient explorer. Keeps its population diverse far longer, which makes it strong at escaping local optima and tackling deceptive, multi-peak landscapes.

03 · Particle Swarm Optimization

Follow the flock

How it works

  • Inertia — particles keep their momentum
  • Cognitive — pull toward personal best
  • Social — pull toward swarm best
  • Velocity — the blend of all three
v ← w·v + c₁·(pbest−x) + c₂·(gbest−x)

Character

Lightweight and elegant. Very few parameters and naturally parallel, but it can converge prematurely when the swarm rushes the same peak.

04 · Differential Evolution

Difference is the engine

How it works

  • Mutation — scale the difference of random vectors
  • Crossover — mix donor with target
  • Selection — keep whichever is fitter
v = x_r1 + F · (x_r2 − x_r3)

Character

The benchmark heavyweight. Self-adapting variants like LSHADE have won CEC competitions and scale reliably to thousands of dimensions.

05 · Ant Colony Optimization

Trails do the talking

How it works

  • Construct — ants walk a graph, building solutions edge by edge
  • Pheromone — each path is marked in proportion to its quality
  • Evaporation — old trails decay so bad routes fade
  • Positive feedback — good edges attract more ants, reinforcing
pij ∝ [τij]α · [ηij]β

Character

The graph specialist. Born for the TSP (Dorigo, 1992), it shines on routing and discrete combinatorial problems where a solution is a path. Variants ACS and Max–Min Ant System sharpen the exploration/exploitation balance.

Beyond the rivalry

Hybrids win in practice

The strongest modern results combine paradigms rather than picking one:

  • GA + DE — fast convergence plus high-dimensional robustness
  • PSO + DE — particle motion with differential mutation (CEC winners)
  • GA + ABC — genetic operators sharpen bee exploitation
  • Memetic (GA + local search) — global reach refined by local polish

Picking one

A quick decision guide

How many dimensions?
<50 → GA · PSO · BCO  |  50–500 → DE · PSO  |  >500 → DE
Need several good solutions?
Yes → BCO  |  No → DE · GA · PSO
Tight time budget?
Yes → DE · GA  |  No → BCO · PSO
Combinatorial / constrained?
Routing / graph → ACO  |  General discrete → GA · BCO  |  Continuous → DE
