# metaheuristics-cram-guide

This repo is a work done with the help of cluade to compact and have the key notes for the course Advance Algorithms, with the context from its slides, some prompts and styleing by me, so its just a qucik review/overview for the topics.

Here are the topics in this repo:

- 1 Introduction — course overview, ill-structured problems Lecture 1
- 2 Problem Complexity — P, NP, NP-complete, SAT, reductions Lecture 2
- 3 Traditional Methods — brute force, greedy, dynamic programmingLecture 3
- 4 Local Search & S-Metaheuristics — neighborhoods, hill climbing Lecture 4
- 5 Random Search — randomized algorithms, Monte Carlo / Las Vegas Lecture 5
- 6 Simulated Annealing — Metropolis, Boltzmann, cooling schedule Lecture 6
- 7 GRASP — greedy randomized adaptive search, RCL Lecture 7
- 8 Tabu Search — memory, tabu list, aspiration, recency/frequency Lecture 8
- 9 Performance Analysis — experimental design, measurement, statistics Lecture 9
- 10 Evolutionary Algorithms — GA, selection, crossover, 8-queens Lecture 10
- 11 Evolution Strategies — real-valued, Gaussian, (μ,λ) / (μ+λ) Lecture 11
- 12 EA Design — representation, crossover, mutation, selection Lecture 12
- 13 EAs for TSP & Memetic — PMX/OX/CX, hybrids, dynamic environments Lecture 13
- 14 Constraint Handling — penalties, repair, rejection, CSP Lecture 14 
- 15 Swarm Intelligence & ACO — stigmergy, pheromone, AS/ACS/MMAS Lectures 15–16
- 17 Particle Swarm Optimization — pbest/gbest, velocity, inertia
